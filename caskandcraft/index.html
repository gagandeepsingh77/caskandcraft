Our Story

Where Craft Meets Class in Haldwani

Nestled in the heart of Haldwani, Nainital, Cask & Craft is a proprietor-run microbrewery & pub — Uttarakhand's gateway to artisanal brewing culture. Every beer is crafted with precision, every dish with passion.

From hoppy IPAs to velvet stouts on tap, and a fusion kitchen spanning Indian spices, Pan-Asian delicacies, and European classics — we're more than a bar near Kathgodam. We're a destination.

On Tap

Our Craft Beers

Six meticulously crafted brews — brewed in-house, served fresh. The best craft beer experience in Haldwani and all of Uttarakhand.

House Fav
🌅

Sunset Saison

Belgian Saison • 5.2% ABV

Bask in golden hues of citrus and a touch of spice. Refreshing, effervescent, perfect for winding down after a long day.

🌑

Midnight Stout

Imperial Stout • 6.8% ABV

Rich, velvety darkness with notes of espresso and dark chocolate. A bold indulgence for those late-night cravings.

Best Seller
🌾

Amber Waves Ale

American Amber Ale • 4.8% ABV

Warmth of summer in every sip. Smooth, balanced, with a hint of caramel sweetness — the perfect everyday companion.

🌿

Hoppy IPA

India Pale Ale • 6.2% ABV

Bursting with tropical and citrus hop aromas. A bold, bitter-forward IPA that satisfies every craft beer enthusiast.

🥂

Crisp Lager

German Lager • 4.2% ABV

Clean, crisp, and endlessly drinkable. Our house lager is the go-to for those who love a classic refreshing pour.

🍋

Tangy Sour Ale

Berliner Weisse • 3.8% ABV

Adventurous and tart, brewed for the bold. Notes of stone fruit and lemon zest make this a unique and memorable sip.
